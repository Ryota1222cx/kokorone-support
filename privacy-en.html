Privacy Policy

Effective: October 17, 2026

Kokorone ("the App") is a conversation practice app provided by Ryota Masuda ("the Operator"). This policy explains what information the App handles, where it is sent, and when it is deleted.

1. Information we collect

  • Account identifier: When you start, the App automatically creates an anonymous account and uses its identifier. On iPhone, if you sign in with Apple, the identifier that Apple issues for you is linked to your account. Signing in is optional and is not required to purchase. We do not receive your email address or your full name.
  • The name you want to be called: Only if you enter it on the first screen, the practice partner uses it to address you. You can continue without entering it. It does not have to be your real name, and we do not treat it as a full name.
  • Conversation records: the text of what you said, the text of what the practice partner (an AI) said, the time of each statement, the inner thoughts that the partner wrote down during the conversation, the scoring results, a record that the crisis support screen was shown (if it was), and the predictions you entered before and after the conversation.
  • Input for practicing before a real meeting: the age range, job, hobbies, and free text you entered about the person you will meet. We delete it when the conversation ends.
  • Analysis of LINE conversations: We send the conversation after names and contact details have been hidden on your device. We do not store it on our server, and we delete it when the analysis is finished. We store only the numbers calculated (such as reply speed) and the advice.
  • Purchase records: the product purchased, the transaction number, the purchase date, the expiry date, and whether a refund was made.
  • Usage counts and costs: the number of practice sessions, and the number of times, the cost, and the time taken when the AI was called.

Your voice is converted to text on your device. We neither store nor send the voice itself. We do not track you for advertising. We have not built in any external analytics tools.

2. Why we use it

To run the conversation with the practice partner, to show you the partner's inner thoughts and scoring after the conversation, to create advice, to manage the state of your purchase, and to fix bugs. We do not use it for any other purpose.

3. Where we send it (including to third parties in other countries)

  • Supabase, Inc.: stores conversation records and accounts in a database in facilities located in Tokyo.
  • Google LLC (a company in the United States), Gemini API: we send the text of the conversation to create the partner's replies, the voice, and the analysis of LINE conversations. We have not specified the country of the facilities that process it. Google's handling follows the Gemini API terms (https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms). Under the paid tier, Google does not use what we send to improve Google's products, and logs it for a limited period only to detect abuse.
  • Apple: purchases on iPhone and Sign in with Apple are handled by Apple's systems.
  • Google: purchases on Android are handled by Google Play. To confirm a purchase, our server asks Google about the purchase token.

4. How long we keep it and how to delete it

From "Delete data and account" in Settings, you can delete your conversation records, the results of LINE analysis, purchase records, consent records, and your account. After deletion, we keep the record of your practice count and of the number and cost of AI calls (to prevent the free sessions from being reset and reused, and to manage cost). These do not contain the content of your conversations.

5. Age

The App is intended for people aged 18 and over.
