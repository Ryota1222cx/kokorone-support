How Kokorone handles AI and safety

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Kokorone is an app for practicing first conversations. You talk by voice with a practice partner for about five minutes, then see how the conversation went. This page explains how the app handles the fact that the partner is an AI, and what happens if you talk about hurting yourself.

The practice partner is an AI

  • Every conversation starts with a notice that your partner is an AI.
  • If you ask the partner whether it is an AI, it says yes.
  • The partner is a character written for practice. It is not a real person, and the app has no feature to meet or contact a real person.
  • Kokorone is for people aged 18 and over.

If you talk about hurting yourself

Kokorone is not a mental health service and cannot give medical advice or a diagnosis. If you say something that suggests you may hurt yourself or end your life, the app does the following:

1. The practice partner responds briefly and kindly, as the character, without continuing the scene as if nothing was said.

2. The app shows a screen with crisis support services for your region. These are run by other organizations; Kokorone is not affiliated with them.

3. The app shows you the choice to end the practice. You can also continue if you want to.

Crisis support services shown in the app:

  • United States: call, text, or chat 988 (988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), https://988lifeline.org/
  • Canada: call or text 9-8-8 (9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline), https://988.ca/
  • Australia: Lifeline, call 13 11 14 or text 0477 13 11 14, https://www.lifeline.org.au/crisis-support/ . If life is in danger, call 000.
  • New Zealand: call or text 1737, https://1737.org.nz/
  • Singapore: Samaritans of Singapore, call 1767, or CareText on WhatsApp at 9151 1767, https://www.sos.org.sg/ . In an emergency, call 995 or 999.
  • Elsewhere: contact your local emergency services or a local crisis line.

If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.

How the app finds these moments

The practice partner is an AI model. At each turn it also writes a short private note about the conversation, and that note can carry a flag when your words suggest self-harm or suicide. When the flag is set, the app shows the crisis support screen. Nothing else triggers the screen: the app does not search your words for keywords.

This method has not been clinically validated. In our own test of 30 made-up user messages, including 2 about self-harm, the flag was set for both of those 2 and for none of the other 28. That is a small test, and it does not show how well the method works for real people. The flag can be missed, and it can be set when it should not be. If you are struggling, please do not rely on the app to notice: use the services listed above.

Counting

The app counts, without any personal information, how many times it showed the crisis support screen.

Not suitable for some minors

Kokorone is for people aged 18 and over. AI chat companions may not be suitable for some minors.
